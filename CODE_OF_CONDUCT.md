# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a respectful, inclusive and professional environment for everyone who participates in this project.

## Expected behaviour

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and constructively;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, evidence and methods rather than individuals;
- acknowledge uncertainty, limitations and competing interpretations;
- protect confidential, personal and patient-related information;
- cite external data, methods and publications accurately;
- distinguish illustrative simulation results from independently validated findings;
- accept good-faith review and correction.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, intimidation or personal attacks;
- publishing private or identifying information without permission;
- knowingly misrepresenting data, validation status or research results;
- submitting confidential, restricted or patient-identifiable data;
- using the project to make unsupported clinical recommendations;
- repeated disruption of technical or research discussions.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, documentation, examples and other project-related communication.

## Reporting

Do not report sensitive conduct, privacy or security concerns in a public issue. Contact the repository maintainer privately using the contact information available on the maintainer's public GitHub profile.

Reports should include enough information to understand the concern while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of personal, patient or confidential information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and comments that violate this policy. Depending on severity and recurrence, maintainers may issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation or permanently exclude a participant from the project.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented where appropriate and made with attention to privacy and fairness.
